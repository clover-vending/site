Questions

FAQ

What if someone doesn't return it?

Their card is charged for the power bank and they keep it. The venue never pays for a lost unit, and we restock the station.

Is it safe?

Yes. The power banks use certified batteries, and every station is insured.

Who fixes it if something goes wrong?

We do. Call or text us and we'll come out. You never pay for repairs.

How much space does it take?

About the size of a toaster. It sits on a counter or bar top and plugs into a standard outlet.
